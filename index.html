About

Hello! I am a PhD student at MIT, co-advised by Professors Collin Stultz and Marzyeh Ghassemi. Prior to my PhD, I received my B.S.E. from Princeton University in 2023, where my senior thesis was supervised by the incredible Professor Christiane Fellbaum.

My research focuses on reliable and controllable machine learning with the goal of building models that behave predictably across diverse conditions. In particular, I find value in working on high-stakes domains like healthcare.

Outside of research, I love reading and birding!

You can reach me at abinitha@mit.edu. I'd love to hear from you!
